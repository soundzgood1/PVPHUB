## v6.6.5 - Season Changes No Longer Delete Data, Backup Archive, Future-Proofing
- REMOVED: The "Start Fresh" button next to Streamer Mode. It existed to clear last season's numbers by hand after a season change — which is no longer necessary now that a season change keeps your data and simply shows it as "(last season)" until each character logs in. What was left was an irreversible, roster-wide delete sitting one misclick away in the main window, with nothing to gain from it. "Reset All Data" under Settings > Advanced is still there for a deliberate full wipe
- CHANGED: A new PvP season no longer deletes anything. Previously PVPHUB cleared every character's ratings, win/loss records and match history the moment it detected a season change — so a single misdetection destroyed the lot, which is exactly what happened when a weekly Conquest cap increase was mistaken for a new season. Last season's numbers are now kept and simply shown as "(last season)" until each character logs in and reports fresh data. A misdetection is now a cosmetic glitch that fixes itself, not data loss
- NEW: A real backup archive, with a browser under Settings > Advanced > "Manage Backups". PVPHUB keeps up to 3 dated snapshots of all your character data. Routine ones are taken at login and at logout and are spaced at least 20 hours apart, so playing several alts in an evening doesn't churn through them; a snapshot is also forced immediately before anything that clears data, regardless of spacing. The window shows what each backup can still recover and restores it with one click — including bringing back characters that are missing entirely. Restoring only fills in what's currently missing; it never overwrites data you still have
  • Also available as |cff00ff00/pvphub backups|r, |cff00ff00/pvphub restore <number>|r and |cff00ff00/pvphub backup|r (create one right now, e.g. before doing something risky)
  • Duplicate snapshots aren't stored twice, and when the archive is full it drops whichever entry sits closest to its neighbours in time rather than the oldest — so a run of resets in one session can't flush a good state from days ago
  • This replaces the old crash-recovery backup as your safety net for deletions: that one is throttled to one copy every 5 minutes, so it cycles completely within an hour of play and was already useless by the time anyone noticed data was gone. It has also been trimmed from 3 slots to 2, since only the newest was ever read — that alone gives back a full copy of your database in file size
- Fixed: PVPHUB used several World of Warcraft functions that Blizzard has deprecated and scheduled for removal. Specialization detection, item counting and the queue-slot lookup now use the current APIs, so Solo Shuffle and Blitz ratings won't break when those old functions go away
- Fixed: The queue-ready sound could permanently lower your game's Sound Effects volume. If the sound played at less than full volume and you reloaded, logged out or disconnected within the next two seconds, the volume was never restored — and each later sound reduced it further. PVPHUB no longer touches your volume setting at all
- Fixed: A memory leak in the Season tab. Every re-render (which happens after each recorded match while the tab is open) permanently accumulated display elements, gradually increasing memory use over a session
- Fixed: Several internal "refresh my ratings now" calls used functions that don't exist, so they silently did nothing. Rating refreshes after a spec change or zone-in now actually reach the server
- Fixed: Hiding the queue timer with /pvpqueue didn't stick — it reappeared as soon as your queue status changed. The command now also works when no queue is active, instead of showing an empty box
- Fixed: PVPHUB re-showed its minimap button every 30 seconds for the whole session, overriding you if you'd hidden it through another addon. Also fixed the icon path used by addon-compartment fallbacks
- Fixed: The "New Specialization Detected" popup kept showing the first spec's name and icon on every later respec
- Changed: PVPHUB now saves a final data snapshot when you log out, so there's always a recent backup to fall back on
- Fixed: The Conquest column and the Conquest total contradicted each other after a season change — the column showed each character's stored amount while the total ignored those same amounts (correctly, since Conquest resets at a season boundary). Stored amounts from last season are now greyed out, and the total shows "—" rather than a misleading "0" when it's empty only because every character's figure predates the new season
- Removed: Four unused PvP item level detection fallbacks, three of which relied on functions that no longer exist; the remaining one used a hardcoded scaling factor that went stale each season

## v6.6.3 Emergency Hotfix - Season Data Wipe Bug
- Fixed: A weekly Conquest cap increase (Blizzard's early-season ramp-up) landing on an ordinary weekly ID reset could be mistaken for a new PvP season, wiping every character's ratings, win/loss records, match history, and MMR history even though the season hadn't actually changed. A weekly ID reset is never a season change — the season-change detection added in v6.6.0 now relies solely on the game's actual season ID, and no longer treats a Conquest cap change as a season boundary on its own
- NEW: If you were affected by the bug above, PVPHUB automatically restores your pre-wipe data from its own internal backup the first time you log in on this version. This only fills in data that's currently missing; it won't touch anything you still have or overwrite an intentional "Reset All Data"/"Start Fresh". If your backup had already rotated past the pre-wipe snapshot by the time you update, that specific match history unfortunately can't be recovered — but your current rating will repopulate as soon as you log into that character
